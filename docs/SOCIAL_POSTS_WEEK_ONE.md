# Bank of Dad - Week One Social Posts

## Post 1 - Founder story

I’ve been building something called Bank of Dad.

It started as a simple idea: make chores, pocket money and saving goals easier to manage at home.

Instead of random pocket money or arguments about whether something was done, children can complete chores, earn XP, unlock badges and save towards rewards. Parents approve each chore before money is added.

It is not a real bank. It is a family reward system with a retro banking-terminal feel.

I’m getting it ready for beta testing now.

Pocket money with purpose.

## Post 2 - Parent problem

Pocket money can get messy fast.

Who did what chore?
How much did they earn?
Did it actually get checked?
What are they saving for?

Bank of Dad is my answer to that.

Children complete chores. Parents approve them. The child earns money, XP, badges and progress towards rewards.

Simple, visual and family controlled.

## Post 4 - Feature post

Bank of Dad currently includes:

- Child accounts
- Chore rewards
- Parent approval queue
- XP and levels
- Achievement badges
- Streaks
- Reward shop
- Savings goals
- Dad Editor mode
- Local browser storage

It is built to make chores feel fairer, more visible and more rewarding.

## Post 5 - Maker angle

Bank of Dad is not just a web app.

The long-term idea is a physical touchscreen family banking terminal using ESP32-S3 hardware.

Imagine a little retro terminal in the kitchen where the kids can check their balance, submit chores and see their savings goals.

That is where I want this to go.
