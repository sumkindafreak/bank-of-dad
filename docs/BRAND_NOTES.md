# Bank of Dad - Brand Notes

## Brand position

Bank of Dad is a retro family reward system for chores, pocket money, XP, badges and savings goals.

It should feel like a fun home banking terminal, not a serious financial service.

## Main slogan

Pocket money with purpose.

## Longer slogan

Where chores become achievements, and savings become adventures.

## Voice

The product should sound:

- Friendly
- Clear
- Encouraging
- Parent-led
- Child-safe
- Playful
- Retro

## Words to use

- Family reward system
- Chore tracker
- Pocket-money tracker
- Parent approval
- Child account
- Reward shop
- Savings goal
- XP
- Badges
- Streaks

## Words to avoid

Avoid wording that suggests Bank of Dad is a real regulated bank, investment tool, payment provider or financial institution.

## Visual direction

The visual style should lean into:

- Dark terminal backgrounds
- Neon green text
- Amber highlights
- Arcade-style panels
- Big clear buttons
- Friendly child-focused progress

## Trust message

Bank of Dad is a household reward tracker. Parents control all chores, balances and rewards.

## Image direction

Useful marketing images:

- Child dashboard screenshot
- Parent approval queue screenshot
- Reward shop screenshot
- Feature map
- Social card
- Physical terminal concept
- App icon
- Facebook cover image

## Tone examples

Good examples:

- Chore submitted for approval.
- Achievement unlocked.
- You are getting closer to your goal.
- Parent approval needed.
- Reward purchased.

Avoid examples:

- You lost against your sibling.
- Your score is bad.
- Real bank account created.
- Investment balance increased.
