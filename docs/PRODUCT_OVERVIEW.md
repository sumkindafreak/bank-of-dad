# Bank of Dad - Product Overview

## One-line description

Bank of Dad is a retro family reward system that turns chores, saving and pocket money into a fun child-friendly banking experience.

## Elevator pitch

Bank of Dad helps children earn pocket money through household jobs, build XP, unlock achievements and save towards rewards in a prize shop. Parents stay in control through an approval queue, editable chores and family-managed accounts.

It is part chore tracker, part pocket-money bank, part reward game and part maker project.

## Core promise

Bank of Dad helps families make chores feel fair, visible and rewarding while teaching children the basics of earning, saving and working towards goals.

## Main users

### Parents

Parents use Bank of Dad to manage chores, approve completed tasks, set reward values and keep pocket money organised.

### Children

Children use Bank of Dad to see what they can earn, track their progress, unlock achievements and save for things they care about.

### Makers

Makers can use the project as a starting point for a physical touchscreen family terminal using the ESP32-S3 firmware.

## Product pillars

### 1. Earning

Children complete approved chores and bonuses to earn money and XP.

### 2. Approval

Parents confirm completed jobs before rewards are added.

### 3. Saving

Children can watch their balance grow and work towards bigger goals.

### 4. Motivation

XP, levels, badges, streaks and bonuses keep the system engaging.

### 5. Family control

The system is designed around parent-managed rules rather than external banking.

### 6. Retro fun

The 1980s banking-terminal style gives the app a personality that feels playful and memorable.

## Current feature summary

- Child dashboard
- Chore list
- Parent approval queue
- Pocket money balance
- XP and levels
- Achievement badges
- Daily streaks
- Savings interest button
- Prize shop
- Kindness bonuses
- No-reminder bonuses
- Dad Editor mode
- Local browser storage
- Optional ESP32-S3 touchscreen terminal firmware

## What makes it different

Most chore apps feel like admin tools. Bank of Dad feels like a family game and a tiny home bank.

The core difference is the combination of:

- Chores
- Pocket money
- Saving goals
- Achievements
- Parent approval
- Retro personality
- Optional physical hardware terminal

## Suggested product positioning

Bank of Dad should be positioned as:

> A fun family banking system for chores, pocket money, goals and rewards.

## Recommended audience

Early marketing should target:

- UK parents with primary-school-age children
- Families already using pocket money systems
- Parents looking for chore charts and reward charts
- Home education groups
- Maker parents who like DIY family tech
- Parenting Facebook groups

## Suggested pricing experiments

These are product strategy ideas only and should be tested with users.

### Free tier

- 1 or 2 child accounts
- Basic chores
- Basic reward shop

### Family tier

- Unlimited child accounts
- Custom chores
- Custom rewards
- Advanced reports
- Export and backup
- PIN protection

### Lifetime early supporter tier

- One-off founder payment
- Access to all future updates
- Recognition as an early supporter

## Risks to handle carefully

- Do not position the app as a real bank.
- Make it clear that it is a family reward and pocket-money tracker.
- Keep children privacy-safe.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary child data.
- Make parent control clear.
- Keep rewards age-appropriate and household-defined.

## Product tone

Bank of Dad should feel:

- Fun
- Fair
- Retro
- Trustworthy
- Family-friendly
- Slightly cheeky
- Easy to understand

## Suggested tagline

> Where chores become achievements, and savings become adventures.
