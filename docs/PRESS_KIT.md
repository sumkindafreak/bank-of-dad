# Bank of Dad - Press Kit

## Product name

Bank of Dad

## Short description

Bank of Dad is a retro family reward system that helps children earn pocket money, build XP, unlock badges and save towards rewards through parent-approved chores.

## Longer description

Bank of Dad is a family chore economy app designed to make pocket money fairer, clearer and more fun. Children complete household tasks, parents approve them, and rewards are added to the child’s account along with XP, levels, badges, streaks and progress towards savings goals.

The project combines a chore chart, reward shop, pocket-money tracker and retro banking-terminal style. It also has an optional hardware direction using an ESP32-S3 touchscreen terminal, turning the idea into a physical family banking station.

## Founder angle

Bank of Dad was built as a practical family project by a dad who wanted a better way to help his children understand chores, earning, saving and rewards.
