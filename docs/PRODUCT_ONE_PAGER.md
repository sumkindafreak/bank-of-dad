# Bank of Dad - Product One Pager

## What is Bank of Dad?

Bank of Dad is a retro family reward system that helps children earn pocket money, build XP, unlock badges and save towards rewards through parent-approved chores.

## The problem

Pocket money and chores can become messy:

- Children forget what they need to do.
- Parents forget what was agreed.
- Rewards can feel inconsistent.
- Saving goals are hard to track.
- Chores often become arguments.

## The solution

Bank of Dad gives each child a simple account with their balance, XP, level, streak, badges, recent activity and savings goal.

Children submit chores for approval. Parents approve or reject them. Approved chores add money and XP to the child account.

## Current features

- Child accounts
- Chore list
- Parent approval queue
- Pocket-money balance
- XP and levels
- Badges
- Streaks
- Savings goals
- Reward shop
- Kindness bonus
- No-reminder bonus
- Interest button
- Dad Editor mode
- Local browser storage
- Optional ESP32-S3 terminal direction

## Why it matters

Bank of Dad helps children understand effort, earning, saving and working towards goals in a way that feels visual and fun.
