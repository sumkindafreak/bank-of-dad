# Bank of Dad - Launch Email Templates

## Beta invite email

Subject: Try Bank of Dad with your family

Hi,

I’m getting ready to test Bank of Dad with a small group of families.

Bank of Dad is a retro family reward system where children complete chores, earn pocket money, collect XP, unlock badges and save towards rewards. Parents approve each chore before money is added, so the system stays fair and controlled.

I’d love you to try it with your family and send honest feedback.

The main things I want to learn are:

- Do children understand it?
- Does it help with chores?
- Does the approval queue make sense?
- Would families keep using it?
- What features are missing?

If you’re interested, reply and I’ll send the beta details.

Thanks,
Toby

## Follow-up email after one week

Subject: How did Bank of Dad go this week?

Hi,

Thanks again for testing Bank of Dad.

Now that you’ve had some time with it, I’d love to know what worked and what didn’t.

A few useful questions:

- Did your child understand how to use it?
- Did they care about XP, badges or rewards?
- Did the approval queue help?
- Was anything confusing?
- What would make you use it every week?

Even short feedback is helpful.

Thanks,
Toby
