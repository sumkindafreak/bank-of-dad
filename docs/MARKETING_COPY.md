## Long product description

Bank of Dad is a family chore economy system built to make pocket money more meaningful. Instead of random rewards or arguments about whether a job was done, children submit chores for approval and parents decide what gets paid.

Each child has their own account with balance, XP, level, streak, badges, savings goal and recent activity. The prize shop gives children something to work towards, while bonuses for kindness and no-reminder behaviour help reward more than just cleaning.

It is designed to teach earning, saving, patience and responsibility while keeping the whole experience fun, visual and parent-controlled.

## FAQ copy

### Is Bank of Dad a real bank?

No. Bank of Dad is a household reward and pocket-money tracker. Parents control all balances, chores and rewards.

### Does it use real money?

It tracks pocket money amounts chosen by the parent. It does not connect to bank accounts.

### Can I edit chores?

Yes. The current version includes Dad Editor mode for chore, reward and child-account changes.

### Is child data shared online?

The current web version stores data locally in the browser using localStorage.

### Can children spend their balance?

Children can use their balance in the reward shop. Parents decide what rewards are available.
