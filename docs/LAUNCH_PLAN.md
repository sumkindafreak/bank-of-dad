# Bank of Dad - Launch Plan

## Launch goal

Turn Bank of Dad from a working family project into a testable public product with a clear message, a beta group and a simple route to paid users.

## Core launch message

> Bank of Dad is a retro family reward system that helps children earn pocket money, build XP, unlock badges and save towards rewards through parent-approved chores.

## Phase 1 - Foundation

### Objectives

- Make the repository presentable.
- Explain the product clearly.
- Prepare marketing copy.
- Create a simple public story around the project.
- Recruit early testers.

### Actions

- Update README with product positioning.
- Add docs explaining the app, launch plan and roadmap.
- Create social copy for Facebook and parenting groups.
- Prepare a beta tester guide.
- Share screenshots and short demo videos.
- Create a waiting list or interest form.

## Phase 2 - Soft beta

### Target audience

- Parents with children aged roughly 5-14
- Families already using chore charts
- Parents who pay pocket money
- Home education groups
- Maker parents
- Friends and local families

### Beta promise

> Try Bank of Dad with your family, use it for real chores and tell us what works, what confuses you and what your children enjoy.

### Beta length

Recommended beta length: 3-4 weeks.

### What to measure

- Do children understand what to do?
- Do parents understand the approval queue?
- Which chores are used most?
- Do children care about XP, badges and levels?
- Do families use the prize shop?
- Does the retro style help or confuse?
- What features do parents ask for first?

### Feedback questions

1. How old are the children using it?
2. Did they understand how to mark a chore complete?
3. Did the approval queue make sense?
4. Did XP, badges and streaks motivate them?
5. Did the prize shop help them save?
6. What felt confusing?
7. What feature would make you use it every week?
8. Would you pay for a polished version?
9. If yes, would you prefer monthly, yearly or one-off pricing?
10. What would stop you from recommending it?

## Phase 3 - Public launch

### Launch channels

- Facebook page
- Personal Facebook profile
- Parenting Facebook groups
- Local community groups
- TikTok / Reels short demos
- Product Hunt-style maker communities
- Reddit parenting and maker communities where allowed
- Local press angle: dad builds retro pocket-money bank for kids

### Launch assets needed

- App screenshots
- 30-second demo video
- 60-second walkthrough
- Simple logo
- Short product description
- Parent benefits post
- Child benefits post
- Beta tester invitation
- Founder story post
- Roadmap image
- Press kit

## Suggested launch timeline

### Week 1 - Prepare

- Finalise docs.
- Record first demo video.
- Create social page.
- Create waiting list form.
- Post founder story.

### Week 2 - Recruit beta testers

- Invite friends and family.
- Post in suitable parenting groups.
- Ask for 10-20 families.
- Collect first feedback.

### Week 3 - Improve positioning

- Review feedback.
- Identify the strongest selling point.
- Collect testimonials.
- Create FAQ from real questions.

### Week 4 - Public push

- Publish demo video.
- Share testimonials.
- Announce beta results.
- Open wider signups.
- Start testing pricing.

## Founder story angle

Bank of Dad has a strong story because it began as a practical family tool, not a corporate app.

Suggested story:

> I wanted a fairer and more fun way to help my children earn pocket money, learn saving and work towards rewards. So I built Bank of Dad: a retro family banking terminal where chores become achievements.

## Recommended first offer

### Early family beta

- Free access during beta
- Direct input into features
- Founder badge or early supporter mention
- Discounted lifetime access if a paid version launches

## Pricing ideas to test

### Option A - Simple one-off

- Free basic version
- £9.99 one-off family upgrade

### Option B - Subscription

- Free basic version
- £2.99/month family plan
- £19.99/year family plan

### Option C - Founder lifetime

- £14.99-£29.99 lifetime early supporter price

## Launch warnings

- Do not overpromise banking features.
- Do not describe it as a real bank.
- Keep child privacy clear.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary personal data.
- Make it clear that parents control all rewards and values.
- Keep the first version simple.

## Best first marketing claim

> Finally, pocket money with purpose.

## Best first call to action

> Join the Bank of Dad beta and help shape a fun family reward system built by a real dad for real families.
